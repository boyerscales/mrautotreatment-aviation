Aircraft detailing, answered

How much does aircraft detailing cost?

Every aircraft is quoted on its own, because size, condition, where it's parked and the services you want all change the price. Call (916) 678-0105, or book a time online and we'll call you with a quote before the job.

Which airports do you serve?

Sacramento Executive (SAC), Sacramento International (SMF), Sacramento Mather (MHR), McClellan Airfield (MCC), Lincoln Regional (LHM), Auburn Municipal (AUN), Yolo County (DWA) and University Airport in Davis (EDU). Parked somewhere else? Call and ask.

Do you wash aircraft with water on the ramp?

Our exterior service is a waterless dry wash with aviation-approved products. It takes off bugs, exhaust film, oil and ramp grime without hoses on the ramp, and it's safe on the paint.

Do I need to be there while you detail it?

No. We coordinate access with your FBO or hangar, photograph the aircraft before we start, and send you after photos when we finish.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Online booking needs 24 hours' notice. If you need it sooner, call and we'll tell you right away what we can do.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. A deposit holds your time and goes toward your total. You pay the rest after the job is done.

What aircraft do you detail?

Piston singles, turboprops, light jets, midsize jets and large-cabin jets. Tell us what you fly when you book, and we'll quote it.
