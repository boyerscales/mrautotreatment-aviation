How it works

Billed after the work, never before

  1. 01

    Pick a plan

    Choose the plan that fits your aircraft and how often it flies.

  2. 02

    Save a card

    On Stripe's secure page. Nothing is charged today, and we never see the number.

  3. 03

    Book your detail

    Online or by phone, any time each month. Members don't pay the booking deposit for their plan's detail.

    Billed after the service

    When your plan's detail that month is done, your card is charged the plan price. No plan detail that month, no charge. Anything outside your plan is quoted and paid like normal.
